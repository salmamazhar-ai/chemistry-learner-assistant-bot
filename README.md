## 🎯 The Problem

Chemistry is one of the subjects students most often abandon mid-way — 
dense vocabulary, multi-step numericals, and equation balancing all 
compound quickly. Most generic AI chatbots either oversimplify the 
chemistry or bury students in jargon, and neither approach actually 
helps a student walk into an exam prepared.

## 💡 The Approach

I designed the bot the way I'd plan a lesson: a clear learning objective, 
a consistent explanatory structure, and a tone calibrated for a real 
classroom — patient, encouraging, and precise. Every instruction in the 
bot's directive was written and tested the way I'd write a rubric: 
specific enough to be reliable, flexible enough to meet a student 
wherever they are.

## 🛠️ Anatomy of the Build

Six configuration layers, each doing a distinct job in how the bot 
thinks, knows, and presents itself:

1. **Instructions** — Core directive defining objective, teaching style, 
   and formatting rules (headings, steps, bullet points)
2. **Knowledge** — Reference base of chemistry content the bot draws on 
   for accurate, curriculum-aligned answers
3. **Logic** — Conversation rules that route a question to the right 
   kind of response — concept, calculation, or safety
4. **Theme** — Owl-tutor avatar, logo, and embed styling — a visual 
   identity that reads as approachable, not clinical
5. **Integrations** — Connection points for deployment — embeddable on 
   a website, LMS, or tutoring portal
6. **AI Model** — Underlying model configuration tuned for accuracy on 
   numerical and equation-based chemistry

## ✅ What the Bot Actually Does

- ✔️ Explains chemistry concepts in plain, student-level language
- ✔️ Solves numerical problems with visible step-by-step working
- ✔️ Balances chemical equations on request
- ✔️ Answers open-ended chemistry questions accurately
- ✔️ Supports quiz and exam preparation
- ✔️ Covers lab experiments and safety guidance
